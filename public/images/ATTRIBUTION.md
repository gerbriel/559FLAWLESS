## Why waxing and skin lightening use abstracts

Deliberate. Stock photos of other people's bodies next to a hair-removal or
pigment-correction service read as before-and-after results, which they are not
— that is misleading at best. Both categories use discreet, on-palette abstracts
until there is real, consented studio photography to put there. Anything showing
a client's body needs a signed photo release; see `consent_forms` (`photo-release`).
